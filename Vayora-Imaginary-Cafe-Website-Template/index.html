A ROOM FOR EVERY KIND OF DAY

Daybook began with a simple thought: not every hour needs to be productive. Some can just be yours. Come in for a flat white, stay because the light is lovely, leave whenever you feel like it.

A neighbourhood table, a small-batch coffee bar, something warm from the oven. Nothing complicated. Everything considered.
